Point your camera at a table of SET® cards and Set Detect instantly finds every valid set for you.

Features:
- Scan a board of cards with your camera and get every valid set highlighted instantly
- Build a hand manually to check whether it's a valid set
- Fully on-device: camera frames are never saved or transmitted

Set Detect works completely offline: no account, no internet connection required, no ads, no tracking, no data collection.
