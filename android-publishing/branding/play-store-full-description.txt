Point your camera at a table of SET® cards and Set Detect finds valid sets.

Features:
- The number of valid sets is shown instantly, but the sets themselves are only revealed on request.
- The app can also be used without a camera. Build a hand manually to check whether it contains a valid set.
- Fully on-device: camera frames are never saved or transmitted.
